1. Information We Collect

The information processed by Puzzle Road depends on the features you use and the services integrated into the Game.

Depending on your use of the Game, information may include:

  • Game progress and level completion information.
  • Stars earned and game scores.
  • Coins, diamonds, hints, and other virtual items.
  • Game settings and preferences.
  • Device and application information.
  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Approximate or coarse location information derived by certain services.
  • Game usage and interaction events.
  • Crash and diagnostic information where applicable.
  • Google Play Games information when you use Play Games features.
  • Purchase-related information required to process digital purchases.

2. Local Game Data

Puzzle Road may store certain game information locally on your Android device.

Local information may include:

  • Completed levels.
  • Stars earned.
  • Coins.
  • Diamonds.
  • Hints and other game resources.
  • Unlocked cities and levels.
  • Game settings.
  • Other information required to maintain game progress.

Local game information is generally stored on your device using Android application storage and/or a local database.

Clearing the application's data or uninstalling the Game may remove locally stored game information.

3. Firebase Analytics

Puzzle Road uses Google Analytics for Firebase ("Firebase Analytics") to understand how players use the Game, improve gameplay, measure performance, and identify technical or usability issues.

Firebase Analytics may automatically collect or process certain information such as:

  • App lifecycle information.
  • Session information.
  • Game interaction events.
  • Device information.
  • Operating system information.
  • Application version.
  • Advertising ID where enabled.
  • Coarse location information derived from masked IP addresses.
  • In-app purchase events.
  • Product-related purchase information such as product ID and price.

The exact information processed by Firebase Analytics can depend on the Game's configuration and the events that we implement.

Firebase states that Google Analytics for Firebase can automatically collect Advertising ID, masked-IP-derived coarse location information, app lifecycle events, and in-app purchase events.

For more information, see Google's Firebase documentation:

Firebase Privacy and Security

Firebase Analytics also provides mechanisms for controlling analytics collection and advertising-related personalization.

More information is available here:

Firebase Analytics Data Collection

8. Google Play Games Services

Puzzle Road may use Google Play Games Services to provide gaming features such as achievements, leaderboards, player profiles, and cloud-based game functionality.

When you use Google Play Games Services, Google may process information associated with your Google Play Games account.

Depending on the features used, this may include:

  • Google Play Games player identifier.
  • Player display name.
  • Player profile information.
  • Achievements.
  • Leaderboard information.
  • Game statistics.
  • Saved-game information.

Puzzle Road uses Google Play Games information only to provide the corresponding game functionality.

9. Google Play Saved Games

Puzzle Road may use Google Play Games Saved Games to allow supported game progress to be saved in the cloud and restored when the player uses the same supported Google Play Games account.

Saved Game information may include information necessary to restore the player's game state, such as:

  • Level progress.
  • Stars earned.
  • Unlocked content.
  • Virtual currency and game resources where included in the save state.
  • Game settings or configuration where included.
  • Other game-state information required for restoration.

Saved Games are provided through Google Play Games Services. Google provides the infrastructure used to store and synchronize supported saved-game data.

Important: Saved Games are separate from the payment system. Puzzle Road does not store credit card numbers, debit card numbers, bank account credentials, or payment passwords in Google Play Saved Games.

Information stored through Google Play Games Services is also subject to Google's privacy practices.

Google Privacy Policy

Purchasable digital products may include:

  • Hints.
  • Boosters.
  • Other virtual items.

Purchases made through Puzzle Road are processed by Google Play's billing system.

Puzzle Road does not directly receive or store your complete credit card number, debit card number, bank account number, payment password, or other complete payment credentials used by Google Play.

Google Play may provide Puzzle Road with purchase information necessary to verify and fulfill a transaction.

This information may include:

  • Product ID.
  • Purchase status.
  • Purchase token.
  • Transaction-related information.
  • Information required to fulfill or restore eligible purchases.

Purchase Verification

Purchase information may be processed to:

  • Verify legitimate purchases.
  • Deliver purchased digital products.
  • Prevent duplicate purchase fulfillment.
  • Detect or prevent fraudulent transactions.
  • Handle refunds or revoked purchases.
  • Restore eligible purchases where supported.
